The lighthouse keeper woke before the sun. A thick fog had rolled in from the sea during the night, and the great lamp needed lighting. She climbed the winding stairs, counting each step as she went. At the top, the glass was cold and damp. She struck a match, and a warm glow pushed back against the grey. Far below, a small fishing boat found its way home, guided by the steady light. The keeper smiled. Every sentence of the sea had its ending, and every sailor deserved a safe one.
